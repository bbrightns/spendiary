# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Single user: Praween. A self-directed investor who manages his own portfolio, DCA schedules, and recurring bank transfers. Uses the app regularly — daily glances on mobile, deeper reviews on desktop. Not a beginner: he knows what the numbers mean and doesn't need the interface to explain money to him.

## Product Purpose

Spendiary is a private, single-user financial cockpit for tracking an investment portfolio (mutual funds, US stocks, Bitcoin), DCA plans, and recurring bank transfer schedules — denominated in Thai Baht. It is not a budgeting app. It is not gamified. Success means Praween can open it and immediately understand where his money stands, what's happening this month, and whether anything needs attention.

## Brand Personality

Premium · Precise · Calm

Quiet confidence. Every number earns its place. The interface feels like a well-made instrument, not a product trying to impress.

References: Apple (restraint, typographic precision), Linear (density without clutter, dark-on-light palette), Notion (calm workspaces), Copilot Money (clean fintech data presentation).

## Anti-references

- **Robinhood / crypto trading apps**: flashy charts, dopamine green/red excitement, confetti on gains, infinite scroll of market data. Spendiary tracks, it does not trade. No excitement mechanics.
- Avoid anything that feels like it's celebrating volatility or nudging behavior.

## Design Principles

1. **Numbers over noise** — every UI element exists to make a number clearer or an action faster. Decoration that doesn't serve the data gets removed.
2. **Private by default** — this is a personal cockpit, not a social or public product. The tone is internal and matter-of-fact, not demonstrative.
3. **Calm confidence** — the interface should never feel urgent unless something genuinely is. Color, motion, and alerts are reserved for things that actually need attention.
4. **Earned color** — color carries semantic meaning (gain, loss, warn, asset class). Using it decoratively dilutes the signal. The palette stays restrained so that when red or green appears, it means something.
5. **Precision over flair** — typographic alignment, number formatting, and data accuracy matter more than decorative elements. A badly formatted number is a design failure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Personal use — best effort. Target WCAG 2.1 AA for contrast and keyboard navigation as a quality floor, but strict compliance is not a hard requirement. Reduced-motion support should be present for any animated transitions.
