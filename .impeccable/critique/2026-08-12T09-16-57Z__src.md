### Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment**:
The interior of Spendiary (Dashboard, Portfolio, DCA Planner, Auto Transfers, Retirement) is remarkably disciplined, using Precision Indigo, clean Plus Jakarta Sans typography, tabular numbers (`.tnum`), and clear information architecture.
However, the **authentication screen** (`App.tsx`) exhibits strong AI slop indicators: radial "cosmic nebula" background gradients, glassmorphism borders (`backdrop-blur-3xl`), glowing circular orbs with `animate-pulse`, and a hover-activated solar flare beneath the primary button. In addition, the Dashboard's Net Worth hero card deviates into dark neo-morphism / neon glow territory (`linear-gradient(135deg, #0b0d14 0%, #151928 100%)` with two `blur-3xl` colored orbs), which conflicts with the strict, restrained product register documented in `DESIGN.md`.

**Deterministic scan**:

### Overall Impression
Spendiary is a thoughtfully built financial cockpit with rich features tailored to Thai investors. The core application feels high-utility and confident. The biggest opportunity is aligning the login screen and dashboard hero card with the minimalist, high-trust fintech aesthetic of the rest of the app, eliminating artificial glow effects and adding power-user keyboard navigation.

---

### What's Working
1. **Financial Domain Depth**: Exceptional support for multi-asset accounting (Sats/BTC, Baht gold/grams, US stocks with real-time THB/USD FX conversion, and Thai retirement runway modeling).
2. **Tabular Data & Hierarchy**: Strict use of `.tnum`, Plus Jakarta Sans for figures/headings, and Inter for metadata creates a legible, high-density dashboard.
3. **Safety & Auditability**: The Holding Activity Log with built-in 1-click Undo gives users complete peace of mind when modifying portfolios.

---

### Priority Issues

#### [P1] Login Screen Aesthetic Dissonance (Cosmic / Glassmorphic Slop)
- **Why it matters**: The login view feels like a generic crypto/web3 AI template with space glows and pseudo-element glass borders. When users log in, they transition into a completely different design language (crisp light/dark fintech).
- **Fix**: Redesign the login view using the same clean card structure, crisp typography, and subtle brand-soft accents as the main cockpit.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable polish` or `$impeccable quieter`

#### [P1] Dashboard Hero Gradient & Neon Glow Orbs
- **Why it matters**: The dark gradient card with neon `blur-3xl` ambient glow orbs violates the product register's rule against decorative background lighting and dark card nesting on light canvases.
- **Fix**: Refactor the Net Worth card into the standard Spendiary card surface with clean typographic hierarchy and high-contrast metric chips.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable quieter`

#### [P2] Missing Keyboard Accelerators for Power Users
- **Why it matters**: Daily wealth tracking requires frequent input. Currently, every modal, search, and tab change requires mouse navigation.
- **Fix**: Introduce keyboard shortcuts (e.g. `n` for new holding, `/` for search, `1-6` for page switching, `Esc` handling everywhere).
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable delight` or `$impeccable harden`

#### [P2] Icon-Only Action Buttons Missing Explicit Accessible Names
- **Why it matters**: Icon buttons in tables and rows (edit pencil, delete trash, chevron dropdowns) rely solely on SVGs without `aria-label`, creating ambiguity for screen reader users.
- **Fix**: Add explicit `aria-label` attributes to all icon-only button elements.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable audit`
